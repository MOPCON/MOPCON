Innovate with New Technologies on Google Cloud

From Site Reliability Engineering(SRE) to Customer Reliability Engineering CRE) and Cloud Ops, there's a lot involved in keeping Google Cloud running, scaling and performing. In this session, Google will give you a close look into the internal procedures we use to continually improve reliability, also discuss best practices for interacting with Google to reduce mean-time-to-detect and mean-time-to-recovery, and talk about how you can apply SRE principles to your organisation.

James Tien
Slide Link

Performant, Readable, Maintainable Java / Android Code

In a software company, the most annoying thing is to read or modify the code left by someone that is overly complicated. What makes it worse is that such code is usually unbeatable compared with any new rewritten code. This session is to discuss the methodology to avoid writing such complex, unreadable, unmaintainable code.

Kuan-Yu Tseng
Slide Link
Something You Don’t Know about Cross-Strait Entrepreneurship

Sharing personal experience of the cross-strait business that from writing the first line of code to establishing the platform with millions of users. The story will be about research and development, product, operation, marketing experiences. In the end, it will analyze the current trends of global entrepreneurial network and some personal views.

JOHN SIE
Shouting out on Google Map, I am in the News!

Talking about the funny thing we can create using LiveMaps on Firebase. Using map project as an example, to share the development experience on Amazon S3, the pros and cons of building LiveMaps on Firebase, and the experience of developing WeatherMaps on Amazon S3.

Slide Link

Improve the design process by customized Sketch Plug-in

UI design is an important part of product development, but in reality, there are too much irrelevant work that distracts designers. We try to improve the tools and automate works for designers to leverage the productivity of designers.

zonble
Slide Link
Product Making, Not Just Arms Race

User research doesn't mean product feature competitive analysis. If you take product feature competitive analysis as user research, then you will only continuously adding new functions on the product just because worrying about losing to its competitors. The number of functions is nothing to with the users as well as the product's utility.

Akane Lee
Agile Transformation and CI/CD of a Team with a Hundred People

After mergers and acquisitions, Hiiir took less than one year to develop friDay shopping platform in 2013. And developing 1 or 2 platforms each year with numerous times of revision. The team faces up to many challenges and perfectly transformed to an agile team with good stability and self-management. They had found the balance between between technical and business needs, and the balance between stability and flexibility of the structure for implementation.

Taien Wang

Cloud！Start from client

Let the developers focus on the developing working. They do not need to worry about system and hardware architecture issues. We cowork with the client to provide the best solution for the client.

Kelvin Chen
Think out of box from UX

A mature App was challenged by the rapid change of technology, people, demand, market, and competitors. “Embrace change” is essential to mobile internet. I will share how to elevate competitiveness and energy for a mature App through the change driven by top-down strategy, acquisition, and UX.

Ivan Wei
How to use VoTT and CNTK to implement video object detection

In this talk we will introduce and demo how to leverage open source VoTT tools and CNTK (Microsoft Cognitive Toolkit) to training a deep learning object detection model , and use it to detect objects in video.

Herman Wu

IoT and AI on AWS

AWS IoT is a managed service designed for Internet of Things and helps to easily and securely connect devices to the cloud. It is capable of extending its connectivity with billions of devices and trillions of messages. This session will discuss the integration of AWS IoT and AI and how to build applications with them.

Pahud Hsieh
What is Blockchains? - from Developers’ Point of View

I will explain what a blockchain is from the developers’ point of view in the talk. A blockchain can be treated as a distributed ledger which provides a consensus between different parties without a central control. Especially on Ethereum, we can execute programs on it. Therefore, I will do a demo on smart contract at the end.

Ming-der Wang
Cross-Disciplinary Hero Project - Risk map of OHCA

During this 4-month project (Data for Social Good,D4SG), we worked with government, clinical experts, and data programmers. We used available data of emergency care and weather and demographic data and adopted the methodology of data sciences, from 3 approaches including "hot zone identification", "risk factor analysis", and "optimization of emergency care," to provide different perspectives about emergency care and clinical practice.

Wei-Yuan Chang

A simple push notification server written in Go.

Gorush is a suite of Push Notification service, which helps programmer not only easily build servers, but also use Command line sending test message to App. This presentation will introduce the operation and framework design of Gorush. The presentation will talk about why choosing Go, the development skills and experience, and how to automatically release version. Back-end engineers that develop mobile phone App or text messaging could not miss this talk!

Bo-Yi Wu
Slide Link
Modern Android Client Architecture

There are many techniques about architecture introduced into Android development communities, also Google has announced its official blueprint samples last year, and guidelines, components for Android architecture in Google I/O this year. This session will talk about how to build up an app with those techniques and libraries.

Shiami Wu
Slide Link
User Interface without "interface"

When VR, AR, and audio service arrive in IOT (The Internet Of Things) world with the transformation from 2D to 3D, product designers are now facing a different challenge and imagination in future.

Rayon Hung

Human Videogame Interaction

Before computers, playing games was almost always a form of social interaction, but the rise of digital games in the latter part of the 20th century allowed them to become solitary activities. How have modern videogames helped bring people together? And how can we design games and software as social experiences?

Ramiro Corbetta

Proof-of-Existence Infrastructure with Blockchain

Blockchain was viewed as the essential skill that changes humankind's digital life after the invention of the Internet. One of its application is proof-of-existence with the property of decentralization and irreversibility. The topic discusses how to build up the fundamental structure of proof-of-existence based on blockchain through mobile devices operating with many nodes and to apply it to digital certificate and identification.

Jim (jserv) Huang

Day by Day, the Road of Developing AR Surgerical Training System

AR market has been blown up after the release of ARkit. The intelligent assistance system in all kinds of professional area seem to be not too far from us. Among them, clinical field has most imaginations since its danger, expensiveness, and rareness. Dr.Huang will use his Augmented Reality Training System as an example to share his experience in cooperation across the disciplines, software-hardware integration, and experience from academic to clinical practice.

Yu-Hsuan, Huang
Brig: Way to release power of love for UI development

Let's write your fantastic cross-platform application with Node.js and Qt! You may wonder how does Tesla make glamorous graphical user interface in products. It's Qt! However, writing application in C/C++ for Qt is not easy for developers. Now, Brig makes it possible to integrate Qt user interface technologies into Node.js.

Development Secret of iOS Camera App and Usage of CameraKit

AppDevKit is an open source development kit with more than 180 features especially for iOS App. In 2017 Camerakit was released to help developers for establishing their own customized photographing services easily. We'll talk about the development process of Camera app, and demo how to use CameraKit to finish camera functions and face-tracking quickly.

Release cycle is getting shorter, and the quality of products has always been the biggest challenge. Specification by Example can be an executable document combined with automated testing to raise the quality, help us to clarify many problems.

On the Mozilla VR team, engineers and designers are working to bring virtual reality to the Open Web, enabling anyone with a web browser to create and surf VR worlds. The hope is that the ubiquity of browsers and the Web will open VR far beyond native VR platforms, to billions of new users.

Play with Webduino - APP x Web x IoT

In the past, Webduino has been an expert in IoT. With the progress of mobile devices, browsers and various technologies, more and more ideas can be done easily. Just 40 minutes, Webduino will take you to experience "App x Web x IoT", and let you feel Webduino is funny and awesome.

OXXO
Animation web page and framework design using Vue/GSAP/d3

The visual design and program development of web page have to be integrated under different frameworks and structures due to different needs of interaction on website. This talk will have case discussions from visual design and web development, integrated with Vue-cli/GSAP/D3 and Vuex to modify and control multipage animations.

Wu Che Yu
Slide Link
Bike Visualization

Bike Visualization aims to transform raw data of bike sharing into readable, usable, and aesthetic ways of information visualization. There are tens of millions of data from the US, Taiwan, the UK, Norway, etc, being analyzed so far. In this session, I will share the journey of creating this website and the challenges I faced.

Chen-Tai Hou
Slide Link

Values and Challenges of a Product Design Team

How to define a design team when UX/UI is talked by everyone? We say design is important, but how to become a design-oriented enterprise? I will discuss challenges and values of a product design team using mobile product design cases.

Kaba Su
React.js, Vue.js. Our choice for the news site.

In Mirror media, we had the React + Redux for the web site presentation in the beginning. After few months, I think maybe we can make it be easier to maintain and develop. It's not a perfect answer, but it might be worth to discuss why the Vue.js is the final solution for us.

Hsin-Chan Chien
Slide Link
AgriWeather - Agricultural Sensors in the Field and Data Science

AgriWeather was developed by using loT and data science skills. AgriWeather is an application of agricultural microclimate analysis. As an angricultural microclimate system tool, it was developed and integrated with data, monitoring, analysis and expert knowledge.

Hsing-Yen Chen
Slide Link

Kaohsiung Map of Fire Risk Assessments 2.0 powered by D4SG

Kaohsiung Map of Fire Risk Assessments 2.0 is developed by D4SG and Kaohsiung City Fire Bureau. This project finds out the key factors of fire incidences through data sciences to optimize the visits to residence buildings with limited fire service resource. We use BalanceCascde to deal with imbalanced learning, several small random forest models to predict the incidence rate of fire, and deep learning models to predict the estimated loss of the fire in residence buildings.

Toby Liu
Explore AI & Machine Learning world inside Google Cloud Platform

Google Cloud is at the forefront of developing cutting-edge machine learning technology. Computer vision, predictive modeling, natural language understanding and speech recognition are among the most popular business use cases of machine learning. In this session, you'll learn how to leverage machine learning and Tensorflow for your own applications.

KJ Wu

Harry Lin
Wonderland of the Internet of Things

Since I started learning IoT, as a hardware person, I think nothing can't be done without thinking from software perspective. As early as I learned ZigBee I knew IoT, and then I found that only firmware is not enough. Later, as I finished writing a ZigBee device driver using Node.js, I again found that it's not enough. After that I stepped on the way learning full stack web development. I hope to introduce some popular IoT communication protocols and my learning experience.

Simen

Tera - a Storage System with Unlimited Capacity

Tera utilizes cloud storage services (Google Drive, SWIFT, AWS S3) and allows Linux-based computers to mount HCFS (Hopebay Cloud Filesystem) to make local storage look as big as the cloud. Th local storage becomes a cache and eliminates the delay from accessing the cloud. Tera is currently available on Android 4.4-7.1.

Ben Jai
